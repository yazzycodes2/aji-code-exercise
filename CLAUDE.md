# Instructions for Claude

This repository is a journalist's personal website. GitHub Pages publishes it from the main branch.

- Commit every change straight to main and push it, so the site updates. Don't open pull requests or leave work on other branches.
- Use my words exactly as I give them: my About text, headlines, outlet names and dates. Don't rewrite, shorten, summarize or add to them. If something is missing, leave a clearly marked spot and ask me.
- Never add facts about me that I didn't give you.
- Keep the site simple: one index.html with its styles inside it, and photos in an images folder. No build tools or frameworks.
- When you finish, tell me in plain words what changed, and that the live site can take a minute or two to update.

## Site style

Keep every change in this style unless I ask to change it.

- **Feel:** quiet, literary and warm, like a writer's site. Lots of empty space, no clutter.
- **Colors:** soft blush background (#fbefec), near-black text (#1f1a1a), warm grey for section labels (#6b5c5a), deep red accent (#a3122a) used sparingly, and a dark block (#2a2626) for buttons.
- **Font:** EB Garamond (from Google Fonts) for everything, with Georgia as the fallback.
- **Top bar:** my name in bold capitals at the top left, with my headline line in small spaced red capitals just under it. Section links (About, Clips, Contact) in spaced capitals at the top right. On phones the links go under my name.
- **Body:** one centered reading column, about 760px wide. Section headings are small, spaced, grey capitals.
- **Links in text:** no plain underline. They get a hand-drawn wavy underline and turn red on hover.
- **Buttons:** a dark rectangular block with light, underlined text, centered. Turns red on hover. My email address is shown this way.
- **Phones:** everything fits a phone screen with 16px side margins and no sideways scrolling.
