About

Yasmeen Khan is a reporter at The Washington Sun.

She graduated from Harvard College with a degree in History and Literature. As an undergraduate, she was the Magazine Chair of The Harvard Crimson and a Ledecky Fellow at Harvard Magazine. She previously interned at The Maine Monitor.
